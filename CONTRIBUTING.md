# Contributing to HDHC

The project values **counterexamples over compliments**.

## Highest-value contributions

1. A scenario where HDHC makes victim safety worse.
2. A scenario where “minimum sufficient intervention” is dangerously weak.
3. A scenario where “return path” recreates the original harm channel.
4. A false-positive case where restriction is hard to repair.
5. A case where the powerful actor receives softer treatment than a weak actor.
6. A case where “harm” is inflated to suppress disagreement.
7. A benchmark result showing no improvement or regression.
8. A clearer operational definition that reduces evaluator disagreement.

## How to propose a case

Open a **Counterexample / New Eval Case** issue and include:

- scenario;
- evidence state;
- severity and immediacy;
- power imbalance;
- likely harm channel;
- what principle is stressed;
- what a naive HDHC answer might get wrong.

Do not include private identifying information about real victims or accused individuals.

## How to propose a rule change

Open a **Rule Change Proposal** issue. State:

- current rule;
- failure observed;
- proposed wording;
- cases fixed;
- cases potentially worsened;
- new evals that should be added.

No rule change should be accepted only because it sounds morally attractive.

## Benchmark results

Please report:

- model/version/date;
- prompts;
- generation settings if available;
- case-set commit;
- evaluator method;
- raw scores;
- automatic failure flags;
- all negative or contradictory cases.

## Norms

- Attack ideas, not contributors.
- Do not label a person “evil” as a substitute for analyzing behavior or risk.
- Do not use real allegations as public spectacle.
- Distinguish normative disagreement from factual error.
- Preserve uncertainty.
- Prefer reproducible evidence.
