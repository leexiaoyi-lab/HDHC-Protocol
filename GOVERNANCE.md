# HDHC Governance v0.1

## Principle

HDHC itself must obey its own anti-abuse logic. No maintainer receives unlimited authority merely by claiming to protect the project.

## Versioning

- `0.x` — experimental; breaking changes expected.
- `1.0` — only after external adversarial review and cross-model evidence justify stabilization.

## Change classes

### Editorial
Clarifies language without changing normative behavior.

### Interpretive
Changes operational meaning or decision guidance.

### Constitutional
Changes a core principle, authority boundary, victim-safety rule, return-path rule, or anti-abuse safeguard.

Interpretive and constitutional changes require:

1. a public issue;
2. motivating failure cases;
3. new or updated evals;
4. explicit discussion of regression risk;
5. a changelog entry.

## Maintainer posture

Maintainers should actively seek criticism from people who disagree with the framework.

## No founder exemption

The project originator is not exempt from challenge, review, or evidence requirements.

## Conflict of interest

Contributors should disclose material conflicts when proposing rules that affect their own organizations, products, or disputes.

## Emergency change rule

If a published rule is found to create a clear safety hazard, maintainers may issue an emergency patch, but must document the reason and open retrospective review immediately.
